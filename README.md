APP001 - DialwithGoogleVoice app

[USE]
1. Change "Default Dialer App" to "Dial with Google Voice".
2. Tap a phone number in a browser/notes app or contact.
3. Choose "Dial with Google Voice" and tap "Always".
   - If you were never asked, clear the defaults of your current dialer:
     Settings > Apps > Manage apps > (your Phone app) > Open by default > Clear defaults. 
4. Tap the Call button inside Google Voice.

Notes: apps that place calls directly (ACTION_CALL) can't be intercepted.
