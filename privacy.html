1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Android application Dial with Google Voice (package name com.example.voiceforwarder), hereafter “the App,” and to no other product or website.

This policy explains what the App does with information. Because the App is designed to operate without any of your information, most of this document consists of describing what does not happen.

2. What the app does

The App is an optional handler for telephone links. When you tap a phone number in another app or in a browser, the App may be offered as a choice. If you select it, the App performs exactly three steps and then closes immediately:

  1. Reads the phone number from the link that launched it.
  2. Formats that number into international dialling form in memory.
  3. Asks Android to open the Google Voice app (or, failing that, your browser) with that number pre-filled.

The App is not itself a dialler. It has no permission to place, receive, or record calls, and it never places a call on your behalf. You always tap Call yourself.

Important limitation Google Voice cannot place emergency calls. If you need emergency services, use your device's built-in phone app directly.

3. Information we do not collect

The App requests no Android permissions at all. Its manifest contains no uses-permission entries. Consequently the App cannot and does not access any of the following:

  • Your contacts, call history, or phone accounts
  • Your location
  • Your files, media, or storage
  • Your device identifiers, advertising IDs, or SIM information
  • Your microphone, camera, or sensors
  • Your notifications or installed-app list
  • The internet — the App has no network access and makes no network requests

The App also contains no analytics, no advertising modules, no crash-reporting libraries, and no code that transmits information to us. It has no backend service. We therefore collect no personal information, no usage statistics, and no diagnostic data of any kind.

4. How phone numbers are handled

The App processes one piece of information: the phone number contained in the link you chose to tap. Here is precisely what happens to it.

Where it comes from

The number arrives inside an Android system intent from the app or web page you were already using. The App does not go looking for phone numbers; it only receives what is handed to it, and only when you choose the App as the handler.

What the App does with it

  • Removes formatting characters such as spaces, dashes, and brackets.
  • Adds a country code where required, so Google Voice can dial it.
  • Places it in a link and passes that link to the Google Voice app, or to your browser if Google Voice is unavailable.

Where it goes next

This is the one point at which the number leaves the App. It is handed to the Google Voice app (or to your default browser), which may then transmit it to Google over the internet in order to show you a call screen and place your call. That transmission is performed by Google's software, not by ours, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

The number is held only in memory for the fraction of a second needed to construct that link. It is never written to disk, never logged, never sent to us, and never retained by the App after the screen closes.

5. Third-party services

The App contains no third-party software development kits. It links to no advertising, analytics, or attribution networks. It communicates with exactly one external application, and only because you asked it to:

  • The Google Voice app and, as a fallback, your default browser. The App simply asks Android to open them with a number pre-filled. Any information they process afterwards is subject to their own terms and privacy policies — see Google's Privacy Policy.

The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Google. Google Voice is a trademark of Google LLC.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

The App creates no data, so there is nothing for us to retain and nothing for us to delete. Specifically:

  • No files, databases, caches, or preference stores are created.
  • Android automatic backup is disabled for this app, so nothing of it is uploaded to cloud backup.
  • No history of numbers you have dialed is kept — not by us, and not by the App.
  • There is no account to close and no server-side record to erase.

To remove the App and all of its data from your device: press and hold the app icon, choose Uninstall, or go to Settings › Apps › Dial with Google Voice › Uninstall. This is the complete and final deletion request; nothing further is required. If you believe any information about you is held by us despite the above, contact us and we will confirm within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because it collects no personal information from any user, no data is gathered from children, nothing is stored, and nothing is disclosed. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information to the App, please contact us — we will confirm that no such information exists.

8. Security

The strongest security measure available is not having sensitive data in the first place. Since the App neither stores nor transmits your information, there is no database, account, or communication of ours that could be breached.

The App runs as an ordinary, sandboxed Android application with no elevated privileges. The phone number it forwards travels through Android's own intent system directly to the app you selected — it does not route through any server controlled by us. Distribution builds are cryptographically signed, so you can verify that the APK you install came from us and was not altered.

9. Changes to this policy

If the App's behaviour ever changes such that it begins to handle information differently, this policy will be updated before or at the same time as that change ships, and the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change. We will not introduce data collection that this policy says does not happen without clearly amending it here. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
